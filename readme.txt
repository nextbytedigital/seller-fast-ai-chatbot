Instantly add an intelligent AI assistant to your WooCommerce store to boost sales and automate customer support.

== Description ==

Seller Fast AI Chatbot is the official auto-installer plugin for Seller Fast users. This lightweight plugin securely connects your WordPress/WooCommerce store with your Seller Fast dashboard. 

With just one click, it injects your customized, multilingual AI chatbot directly into your website's footer, allowing you to provide 24/7 automated customer support, answer product queries, and drive more conversions.

= Features =
* 1-Click Installation: No complex coding or API configuration required.
* Lightweight & Fast: Does not slow down your website.
* WooCommerce Ready: Perfectly optimized for WooCommerce stores.
* Secure Connection: Uses strict standards to communicate with your Seller Fast backend.

== Installation ==

1. Go to your Seller Fast Dashboard and generate your magic connection link.
2. Install and activate this plugin.
3. The AI Chatbot will automatically appear on your website based on your Seller Fast settings.

== External services ==

This plugin connects to a proprietary external webhook (n8n.sellerfast.tech) to process chatbot messages and retrieve store-specific product data.
It sends the user's chat messages and the website's root URL (Store URL) whenever a user interacts with the chatbot to accurately identify the store and fetch product answers.
